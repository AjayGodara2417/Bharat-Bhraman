# 🌏 Bharat-Bhraman

**Bharat-Bhraman** is an innovative platform designed to revolutionize the tour and travel experience in India. Whether you're a solo explorer or planning a family trip, Bharat-Bhraman provides a seamless, AI-powered solution to plan your journey across the most beautiful destinations in India.

---

## 🚀 Features

- **Personalized Itineraries:** Enter your starting location, budget, and travel theme (temples, gurudwaras, hill stations, historical sites, and more).
- **AI-Generated Roadmaps:** Instantly receive a detailed travel plan covering all must-visit places based on your preferences.
- **State-wise Exploration:** Choose your desired state and discover hidden gems and popular attractions.
- **No Middlemen:** Avoid expensive tour packages and guides—explore at your own pace, your own way.
- **Comprehensive Details:** Get all the information you need about each destination, including travel tips, best times to visit, and local insights.

---

## 🎯 Why Bharat-Bhraman?

- **Empowering Travelers:** Take control of your journey with customized plans.
- **Boosting Indian Tourism:** Showcasing the diversity and beauty of India to the world.
- **User-Friendly:** Simple, intuitive interface for travelers of all ages.

---

## 🛠️ How It Works

1. **Input Your Details:** Starting location, budget, and travel theme.
2. **Select a State:** Choose where you want to explore.
3. **Get Your Plan:** Receive an AI-generated roadmap with all the details you need.
4. **Travel Smart:** Visit all your favorite places efficiently and economically.
